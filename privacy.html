Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Purpose and scope

Jarvis is a privately operated personal-assistant application. It is not offered to the general public. This policy explains how Jarvis handles information received through Google APIs.

Information accessed

Jarvis accesses only the Google account data covered by permissions that the account owner explicitly grants on Google's consent screen. Depending on the features enabled by the owner, this may include Gmail messages and metadata and other Google service data identified on that consent screen.

How information is used

Google account data is used solely to provide functions requested by the account owner, such as finding, summarizing, organizing, drafting, or carrying out authorized tasks. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale.

Sharing and disclosure

Google account data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own purposes. It may be processed by infrastructure providers only where necessary to operate the application, subject to appropriate safeguards, or disclosed when required by law.

Storage, security, and retention

Authorization credentials and any locally retained data are protected using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Data is retained only for as long as needed to provide the requested functions or meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Control and deletion

The account owner can revoke Google access at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion can be sent to jarvis.grnec@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's capabilities or legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will remain available on this page.
